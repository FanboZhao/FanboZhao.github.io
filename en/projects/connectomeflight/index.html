ConnectomeFlight: bio-inspired embodied navigation

Ongoing, unpublished research into bio-inspired vision, multimodal perception, and embodied navigation, with companion visualization tools.

On this page
  • Research interests
  • Companion visualization tools
  • Research status

Ongoing research · Unpublished

ConnectomeFlight explores how biological vision and multiple senses can inform perception and action in embodied agents, using a simulated fruit fly. The project brings together simulation-based research and visualization tools to investigate connections between bio-inspired intelligence and embodied navigation.

Research interests

  • Bio-inspired vision: the organization of biological vision and its relevance to perception and behavior in artificial agents.
  • Multimodal navigation: how multiple senses can jointly help an agent understand its environment and find a target.

Companion visualization tools

ConnectomeFlight-Web supports the organization and inspection of perception and behavior in simulation, helping with research review and communication. It is a companion tool within the same research project.

Research status

This research has not yet been published. This page provides a high-level introduction; methods, experimental materials, and results will be shared at an appropriate stage.
