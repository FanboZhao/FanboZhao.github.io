RESEARCH & PROJECTS

Hi, I’m Fanbo Zhao.

I’m an AI student at Fudan University, interested in bio-inspired intelligence and multimodal perception. Through ConnectomeFlight, I explore how biological vision and multiple senses can inform embodied navigation.

Research interests

Bio-inspired vision and embodied intelligence ↗

How can the organization of biological vision inform perception and action? I’m interested in links between biological insights and embodied tasks.

Multimodal perception and navigation ↗

How can multiple senses help an agent understand its environment and find a target? I explore these questions through navigation in simulation.
