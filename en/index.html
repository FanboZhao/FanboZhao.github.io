CURRENT INTEREST / 01

Multimodal embodied navigation

How can vision, odor, and wind support autonomous navigation? I explore reinforcement learning, recurrent memory, and hierarchical control in simulated fly flight.

SmartFly aims to support visual obstacle navigation and vision–odor source seeking with a single policy. Full navigation performance remains under evaluation.

CURRENT INTEREST / 02

Biologically inspired visual representations

Comparing CNN and FlyVis visual encoders to investigate how visual priors relate to representations and behavior in embodied tasks.

The project includes shared-stimulus, trajectory-level probing, and closed-loop evaluation workflows. The full comparison across training seeds is still pending.

01 / 2026In progress

SmartFly

Fly-inspired multimodal embodied navigation

A dual-task navigation framework in a MuJoCo fly simulation, combining visual obstacle navigation and vision–odor source seeking, with CNN–RNN and FlyVis–RNN encoder variants.

The project spans hierarchical PPO control, forest and dynamic odor environments, curriculum training, and representation analysis. CNN–RNN stabilization replays are available in both observation modes; the full navigation comparison is ongoing.
