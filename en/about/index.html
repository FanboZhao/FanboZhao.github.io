Research interests

I’m interested in bio-inspired intelligence, multimodal perception, and embodied navigation: how biological insights and multiple senses can help agents perceive and act in their environment.

My current work centers on ConnectomeFlight, with companion visualization tools. This research is unpublished, and the website provides only a high-level introduction.
