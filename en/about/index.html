About me

A second-year AI student at Fudan University, interested in embodied intelligence and multimodal perception.

Hi, I’m Fanbo Zhao, a second-year undergraduate in artificial intelligence at Fudan University.

My interests include embodied intelligence, reinforcement learning, multimodal perception, and biologically inspired vision. I’m particularly interested in how sensory information helps an agent act in its environment.

My current project exploration centers on SmartFly: fly-inspired navigation in simulation, combining visual, odor, and wind information, with a planned comparison of CNN and FlyVis visual encoders. This is ongoing work; the project page distinguishes implemented components and stabilization replays from research questions still awaiting evaluation.

This website is a record of my research exploration, AI projects, and learning notes. I’m looking for research and internship opportunities, and I’d love to connect with people who share an interest in AI.

Alongside technical work, I hope to share everyday observations and reflections here.
